From the Desk of Pastor Curtis

From the Desk of Pastor Curtis

As we begin this new season, I'm reminded of the Kingdom's unrelenting call to transformation. Our monthly theme, Kingdom Education, is not just about academic pursuits, but about cultivating a worldview that sees the world through the lens of Jesus' lordship. Today, I want to encourage you to develop a Kingdom worldview that impacts every area of your life.

In Matthew 6:33, Jesus instructs us to "seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you." This verse is not just a call to prioritize spiritual growth, but to see every area of life – education, economics, relationships, and more – as a domain where Jesus is Lord. When we recognize this, we begin to approach education with a Kingdom perspective, seeing it as a means of cultivating wisdom, justice, and compassion.

As we engage in Kingdom education, we must also recognize the role of the Holy Spirit in empowering us to live as agents of transformation. In 1 Corinthians 2:14, Paul writes, "The person without the Spirit does not accept the things that come from the Spirit of God for they are foolishness to him and he cannot understand them, because they are spiritually discerned." As we deepen our understanding of the Kingdom, we must also seek the Spirit's guidance and empowerment to apply it in our lives.

So, how can you apply this Kingdom worldview in your daily life? Start by asking yourself questions like, "How can I use my education to serve the marginalized and oppressed?" or "How can I cultivate a learning environment that honors Jesus as Lord?" As you seek to develop a Kingdom worldview, I encourage you to be bold, be prophetic, and be a catalyst for transformation in your sphere of influence.

May the Lord give you wisdom, courage, and a hunger for the Kingdom as you seek to live out your faith in the midst of everyday life.

Pastor Curtis

Kingdom Intelligence

Global Church Growth: A New Era of Gospel Expansion

The global church is witnessing unprecedented growth, with an estimated 100,000 new churches planted worldwide each year. This surge is attributed to the rise of independent, non-denominational churches, particularly in Africa and Asia. According to a recent report, the number of Christians in Africa has doubled in the past two decades, with the continent now home to over 500 million believers. This trend is a testament to the power of the gospel to transform lives and communities.

Kingdom Perspective: As Jesus' lordship over all nations is proclaimed, His church is expanding its reach, demonstrating the unstoppable growth of the Kingdom of God.

Persecuted Church: A Beacon of Hope

In the face of increasing persecution, the church in China is demonstrating remarkable resilience. Despite the government's efforts to suppress Christianity, the number of underground churches continues to grow, with estimates suggesting over 100 million believers. These churches are not only providing a sense of community and hope to their members but also serving as a witness to the government's human rights abuses. This remarkable story highlights the church's ability to thrive even in the darkest of circumstances.

Kingdom Perspective: As Jesus' people face persecution, they remain a beacon of hope, shining brightly in the darkness, and proclaiming the lordship of Jesus over all authorities.

Prayer Movements: A Global Wave of Intercession

A global prayer movement is sweeping the world, with Christians from diverse backgrounds coming together to pray for revival, healing, and transformation. From the United States to Africa, Asia, and Europe, prayer gatherings and online initiatives are uniting believers in a common pursuit of God's presence and power. This movement is a manifestation of the Holy Spirit's empowerment, as believers are called to be agents of Kingdom transformation, praying for the advancement of God's rule on earth.

Kingdom Perspective: As believers unite in prayer, they are unlocking the power of the Holy Spirit, who empowers them to live as agents of Kingdom transformation, influencing every sphere of society with the lordship of Jesus.

Kingdom Living

Kingdom Living: Embracing Kingdom Education in Every Sphere

As we explore the theme of Kingdom Education this month, it's essential to remember that true education happens not just in a classroom, but in every moment of our lives. As Kingdom citizens, we're called to learn, grow, and influence in every sphere of society - from our homes and workplaces to our communities and relationships. Here's how you can live out Kingdom Education in practical ways this month:

In the Home: Intentional Conversations

This month, make an effort to engage in conversations with your family and friends that spark curiosity and encourage Kingdom thinking. Ask questions like: "What does it mean to be a Kingdom citizen in our community?" or "How can we apply biblical principles to our daily decisions?" Use this opportunity to model Kingdom thinking and create a culture of learning and growth in your home.

At Work: Seeking Wisdom

In the workplace, seek out wisdom from colleagues and mentors who embody Kingdom values. Ask for their insights on how to apply biblical principles to your job or industry. This will not only help you grow professionally but also foster a culture of Kingdom influence in your workplace.

In the Community: Serving with Purpose

Look for opportunities to serve your community in ways that demonstrate Kingdom values. Volunteer with a local organization or participate in a community event that promotes Kingdom thinking. This will not only bring blessing to those around you but also create a platform to share the Good News of Jesus' lordship.

In Relationships: Empowering Others

This month, look for opportunities to empower others by sharing your knowledge and gifts. Whether it's mentoring a younger believer or serving on a committee at your church, use your skills to help others grow and develop in their faith. Remember, Kingdom Education is not just about personal growth, but also about equipping others to live out their Kingdom calling.

Scripture to Meditate On:

"Come, let us go up to the mountain of the Lord, to the house of the God of Jacob; that he may teach us his ways, and that we may walk in his paths." - Isaiah 2:3

May this month's focus on Kingdom Education inspire you to live out your Kingdom calling in every sphere of society. Remember, true education is not just about acquiring knowledge, but about applying biblical principles to every area of life.
